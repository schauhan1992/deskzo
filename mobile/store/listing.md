## Full description (4,000 characters)

> The Deskzo app is for people whose company runs on Deskzo. Pair your phone once, switch on call sync, and every call with a customer is logged in Deskzo by itself — no form to fill in after you put the phone down.
>
> **Your calls, logged for you**
> After each call with a customer or contact in Deskzo, the app sends the number, the time and how long it lasted. Deskzo puts it on the customer's history, on the lead it was about, and in your team's calling report. Personal calls are checked and dropped — they are not stored.
>
> **Know who's calling**
> When a customer rings, their name, their company and the deal they're on show while it rings.
>
> **A word on how it went**
> A tap on "How did it go?" after the call: connected, callback requested, not interested — a note, a callback time and the lead it was about. A callback becomes a task with a reminder.
>
> **Never miss a callback**
> A customer's missed call puts a callback on your list. The app's home screen shows the callbacks you owe, with the number one tap away.
>
> **Ring my phone**
> Working in Deskzo on a computer? Press "Ring my phone" on any contact, and your phone offers to dial it — the call logs itself when it ends.
>
> **In your company's control**
> The app signs in to your company's own Deskzo with a code from your Settings — no new password. Your administrators see paired phones in Security › Devices, can require their approval and can sign one out at any time. You can switch call sync off, or sign out, whenever you like.
>
> **What it doesn't do**
> It doesn't record calls, read your messages or your phone's contacts, track your location or show ads.
>
> You need a Deskzo account from your company to use the app. Learn more at deskzo.com.
