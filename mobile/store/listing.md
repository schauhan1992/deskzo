## Full description (4,000 characters)

> The Deskzo app is for people whose company runs on Deskzo. Sign in once, and your working day is in your pocket: clock in, tick off today's tasks, decide what waits for you, ask for leave. Switch on call sync, and every call with a customer is logged in Deskzo by itself — no form to fill in after you put the phone down.
>
> **Your day at a glance**
> Clock in and out with a tap — where you were is saved with your attendance, if you allow it. See the tasks due today and tick them off. Approve or reject leave, expense claims, attendance corrections and other requests waiting for you. Check your leave balance, and ask for leave on a calendar — Deskzo works out the days, weekends and holidays left out.
>
> **Team chat**
> Chat with your colleagues as you would on WhatsApp — one to one or in groups: replies, reactions, photos and files, read ticks, "typing…", who's online. It stays inside your company's Deskzo, under your company's rules.
>
> **All of Deskzo**
> Everything else in your company's Deskzo opens in the app, already signed in.
>
> **Your calls, logged for you**
> After each call with a customer or contact in Deskzo, the app sends the number, the time and how long it lasted. Deskzo puts it on the customer's history, on the lead it was about, and in your team's calling report. Personal calls are checked and dropped — they are not stored.
>
> **Know who's calling**
> When a customer rings, their name, their company and the deal they're on show while it rings.
>
> **A word on how it went**
> A tap on "How did it go?" after the call: connected, callback requested, not interested — a note, a callback time and the lead it was about. A callback becomes a task with a reminder.
>
> **Never miss a callback**
> A customer's missed call puts a callback on your list. The app's home screen shows the callbacks you owe, with the number one tap away.
>
> **Ring my phone**
> Working in Deskzo on a computer? Press "Ring my phone" on any contact, and your phone offers to dial it — the call logs itself when it ends.
>
> **In your company's control**
> You sign in on your company's own Deskzo page — with its password, two-factor or single sign-on — or with a code from Settings on a computer. No new password. Your company's sign-in rules apply, and your administrators see the app in Security › Devices, can require their approval and can sign one out at any time. You can switch call sync off, or sign out, whenever you like.
>
> **What it doesn't do**
> It doesn't record calls, read your messages or your phone's contacts, or show ads. It reads your location only when you clock in or out, or check in and out of a customer visit, if you allow it (or sign in, if your company asks for that) — never in the background.
>
> You need a Deskzo account from your company to use the app. Learn more at deskzo.com.
